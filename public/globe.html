What this map can and cannot tell you

Loading data provenance…

The damage model was trained on the 2015 Gorkha survey (Nepal) and its inputs are building attributes, not coordinates. A prediction here therefore describes the building profile you enter, not the real structure standing at that point.

The globe shows country outlines, not individual buildings: no worldwide footprint set is packaged here. The Example button scores 30 real held-out survey buildings and checks them against the grade the survey recorded.

Hazard colours rank where magnitude 5.5+ events have clustered since 2000. Catalogue completeness varies by region and decade. Not a forecast, not a safety rating, not a design map.

Model: damage-model-v1, a LightGBM and XGBoost probability average trained on the 2015 Gorkha survey (75.68% accuracy, macro-F1 0.7099). Survey location IDs are fixed to dataset defaults, because coordinates cannot be mapped onto them. The grade is a statistical estimate, not a structural assessment.
