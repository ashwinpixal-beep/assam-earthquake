About the data and its limits

Boundaries are the public Survey of India state outlines (MIT-licensed set), snapped to a 0.02° grid by scripts/build_india_states.py: 36 states and union territories, 29,503 points.

Epicentres are the bundled USGS catalogue for the India region, M4.0+ from 2000-01-01 to 2026-10-26 (18,501 events). Only the events that fall inside an Indian outline are counted per state — 1,463 of them; the rest are Afghanistan, China, Nepal, Myanmar and the seas around India.

Only Assam has packaged building footprints (837 tiles, 13,302,299 buildings, Overture Maps). Every other state is shown from the earthquake record. Nothing is invented.

Footprints © Overture Maps Foundation (ODbL). Boundaries from the Survey of India set (MIT). Catalogue: USGS. Model: damage-model-v1 (LightGBM + XGBoost ensemble), trained on the 2015 Gorkha survey.
