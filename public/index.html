About the data and its limits

Footprints carry no survey attributes, and the dataset has no coordinates. So the predicted grade depends on the inputs in the panel, not on the building's shape or position. Use this map to explore scenarios, not to assess a real building.

Heights are the 4 m estimated default unless the source records a height. Only 77 buildings have a recorded height.

Building footprints © Overture Maps Foundation, released under ODbL. Boundary: Overture divisions (IN-AS). Model: damage-model-v1 (LightGBM and XGBoost ensemble), trained on the 2015 Gorkha survey.
