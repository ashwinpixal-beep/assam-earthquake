Where the building model fits

The damage model needs building attributes, not coordinates, so it runs the same anywhere. The 3D places view runs it for Assam (13.3 million real footprints), Kutch and Koyna, and the Example panel there scores 30 real held-out survey buildings against the grades the survey recorded.
